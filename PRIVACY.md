# Privacy notice

## If you use the Muse prompt (Option A)

- Your conversation happens between **you and your own Muse account**.
  Sign up at the official Muse site/app and use a new private chat.
- What you share (answers, paystub photos, notes) is governed by **Muse's
  own privacy policy** — read it before sharing sensitive documents.
- The person who shared the prompt (Zach) **never sees any of it**. There
  is no shared account, no shared folder, no admin dashboard.
- One coworker cannot see another coworker's chat.
- Practical safety: use your phone's lock screen; don't share your Muse
  login; don't paste passwords. You can delete a Muse chat any time.

## If you use the text guide (Option B)

- Nothing is collected at all. It's a document you read. Your notes stay
  wherever you keep them.

## What this project never asks for

- Your QuickBooks password or any account login.
- Customer invoices, reservation histories, POS exports, or anyone else's
  pay records.
- Anything you wouldn't be comfortable showing the Labor Commissioner —
  because that's who your evidence is for.

## What stays with you

Your paystubs, your notes, your messages, your statement. Keep copies
somewhere safe (your phone, your email, a folder at home). If you file,
the Labor Commissioner gets copies — never originals.
