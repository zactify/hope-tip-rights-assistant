## MESSAGE 1 — Zach's note (send first)

Hey everyone, it's Zach. As of today (Oct 14) I've resigned from HOPE — and
I want to be honest with you about one of the main reasons.

I've seen records that make me believe customer tips left for staff haven't
been reaching the people they were left for. I'm NOT sharing those records,
and I need you to understand why: they contain customer names, payment
details, and other people's private info. Passing them around could get me
in legal trouble and could put YOU at risk too. What I can tell you is what
I saw with my own eyes — and I believe you deserve every dollar and more.

I have nothing to gain from this. I'm not in the tip pool, I don't get a
cut of anything, and I'm already gone. I just couldn't keep working
somewhere I didn't trust on this.

If you want to look into it for yourself, you have two options:

OPTION A — Your own private AI helper (easiest): sign up for Muse (free),
open a new chat, and paste the prompt I'm sending in my next messages.
It'll interview you privately, help you pull your paystubs, organize your
evidence, and draft everything for a California wage claim. Everything you
share stays between you and your Muse — I never see any of it.

OPTION B — Do it yourself with the text guide (no AI needed):
https://github.com/zactify/hope-tip-rights-assistant/TEXT_GUIDE.md — the steps, what evidence you need, and what to
expect if several of us file.

A few honest things: I can't promise anyone money or an outcome. But
several people filing independent, truthful claims about the same pattern
is much harder to ignore than one person. You don't have to quit to file.
Retaliation for filing is illegal.

I genuinely enjoyed working with all of you. You deserve to be paid fairly
and treated fairly — and if HOPE won't do that, you can and should find
somewhere that will.

Questions about any of this? Message me privately — what you tell me stays
between us, unless you decide to share it yourself.

— Zach
